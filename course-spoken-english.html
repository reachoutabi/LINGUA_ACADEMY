12-Week Syllabus Breakdown

  • Overcoming hesitation, basic greetings, and polite conversational phrases.
  • IPA vowels, consonants, word stress, and rhythm basics.
  • Eliminating filler words ("um", "ah", "like") through pause management.

  • Ordering food, asking for directions, shopping, and travel scenarios.
  • Storytelling: Narrating past experiences, anecdotes, and opinions.
  • Telephone etiquette and casual group discussions.

  • Conducting business introductions, attending meetings, and pitching ideas.
  • Diplomatic language, giving constructive feedback, and handling disagreements.
  • Writing crisp professional emails and Slack summaries.

  • Impromptu speech strategies: The PREP formula (Point, Reason, Example, Point).
  • Advanced debate simulations and group discussions.
  • Graduation presentation with comprehensive CEFR B2 assessment report.
