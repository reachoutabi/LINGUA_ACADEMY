Designed For Your Routine

Choose Your Learning Cadence

Whether you are balancing full-time employment, university studies, or personal commitments, our flexible batch formats ensure consistent progress.

Most Intensive

Weekday Fast-Track

Daily immersive sprint designed for students aiming for rapid CEFR level upgrades or upcoming visa/interview deadlines.

  • Schedule: Mon – Fri (5 Days/wk)
  • Duration: 90 Mins per session
  • Batch Size: Max 10 students
  • Pace: 1 Full CEFR Level in 6 Weeks
Most Popular

Weekend Immersion

Comprehensive deep-dive workshops for busy professionals who want intensive language acquisition without weekday stress.

  • Schedule: Saturday & Sunday
  • Duration: 3.0 Hours per day (with breaks)
  • Batch Size: Max 12 students
  • Pace: 1 Full CEFR Level in 10 Weeks
After-Work Slot

Late Evening Pro

Post-office interactive conversational classes tailored specifically for IT engineers, executives, and consultants.

  • Schedule: Tue & Thu / Mon & Wed
  • Duration: 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM
  • Batch Size: Max 8 students
  • Pace: 1 Full CEFR Level in 12 Weeks
Zero-Risk Learning

Never Miss a Single Lesson

Life gets unpredictable. Our student flexibility policies ensure your language progress never stalls due to busy work weeks or travel.

HD Session Recordings

Every live online session is recorded in HD with searchable transcripts and available in your portal within 2 hours.

Free Batch Switching

Switch from morning to evening, or pause your enrollment for up to 6 months with zero penalty or administrative fees.

1-on-1 Catch-Up Clinics

If you miss a crucial grammar or phonetic module, book a 15-minute 1-on-1 clinic with your mentor to stay caught up.

Daily Speaking Clubs

Hop into peer breakout rooms and trainer-moderated open discussion circles 7 days a week for extra speaking practice.
