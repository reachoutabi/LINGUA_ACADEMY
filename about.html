Our Purpose

Breaking Language Barriers, Unlocking Human Potential

Founded in 2014 by a group of passionate linguists and corporate communication strategists, Lingua Academy was built on a simple premise: traditional language learning focuses too heavily on rote grammar drills and not enough on active speech confidence.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-energy, CEFR-aligned language coaching that empowers learners of all ages to speak with absolute clarity.

Our Vision

To be the world’s most trusted multi-skill communication academy, bridging cultural divides across international borders.

The Lingua Methodology

Our 4 Core Teaching Pillars

How we guarantee natural fluency, accent clarity, and lasting communication confidence.

80% Speaking Immersion

Every session is 80% conversational debate, impromptu presentation, and structured dialog roleplay.

Max 12 Cohort Guarantee

Strictly capped batch sizes ensure every student speaks for at least 15 minutes in every single class.

Weekly Accent Diagnostics

Personalized phonetics feedback to eliminate mother-tongue influence and refine cadence.

CEFR International Diplomas

Formally recognized language diplomas accepted by foreign universities and MNC employers.
