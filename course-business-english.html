10-Week Executive Syllabus Breakdown

  • Executive summaries, proposal drafting, and bulleted status briefings.
  • Diplomatic escalation, saying "No" gracefully, and resolving email misinterpretations.
  • Modern workplace etiquette: Slack, Teams, and asynchronous messaging norms.

  • Setting agendas, steering heated debates, and timebox management.
  • Interrupting politely, defending data points, and building cross-functional consensus.
  • Virtual meeting mastery: Web conferencing body language and microphone presence.

  • BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement) and value-based phrasing.
  • Handling client pricing objections and structuring win-win scope adjustments.
  • Delivering product demo pitches and ROI-centric closing statements.

  • High-context vs low-context cultural communication nuances.
  • Crisis communication and corporate brand reputation management.
