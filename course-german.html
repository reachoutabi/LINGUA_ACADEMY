16-Week Syllabus Breakdown

  • Pronunciation, umlauts (ä, ö, ü), and definite/indefinite articles (der, die, das).
  • Akkusativ case, modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen), and daily routines.
  • Asking questions, shopping, dining out, and time management.

  • Dativ case, two-way prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen), and directional logic.
  • Perfekt tense with haben and sein, Partizip II rules.
  • Housing in Germany, public transport, and navigating official city registrations (Anmeldung).

  • Nebensätze: weil, dass, wenn, obwohl, and verb-kick placement.
  • Konjunktiv II for polite requests (hätte, wäre, würde).
  • Workplace vocabulary: IT, engineering, nursing, and business terminology.

  • Hören (Listening) audio traps and rapid answer marking.
  • Schreiben (Writing): Formal letters, complaint emails, and forum essays.
  • Sprechen (Speaking): Partner presentation and debate simulations with certified evaluators.
