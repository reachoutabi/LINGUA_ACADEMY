What Your Child Will Gain

Natural Phonics & Diction

Learn crisp articulation, accurate vowel sounds, and clear expressive speech.

10-Week Junior Syllabus Breakdown

  • Tongue twisters, mouth muscle games, and phonemic awareness.
  • Polite greetings, introduction games, and meeting new friends with a smile.
  • Describing favorite toys, superheroes, and pets with emotion.

  • "Show & Tell" workshops with interactive physical and digital props.
  • Sequencing stories: Beginning, middle adventure, and exciting conclusion.
  • Using descriptive adjectives (sparkling, gigantic, mysterious, cheerful).

  • Mini roleplay skits: Supermarket, space mission, wildlife safari, and news anchor.
  • Gentle debate: "Books vs Video Games", "Summer vs Winter" with reasons.
  • Listening to peers politely and taking turns in group conversation.

  • Body language, confident smile, eye contact, and posture practice.
  • Preparation for the 3-minute Junior Ted-style talk on their passion project.
  • Live Parent Showcase event with certificates, feedback, and celebration medals.
